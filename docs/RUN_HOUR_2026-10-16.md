## What remains unproven

No independent ground-truth stream was collected, so the missed-event rate and
network-wide coverage are unknown. No 24/7 availability or delivery SLA follows
from one uninterrupted hour. Recovery remains bounded and does not reconstruct
historical account updates. External pilot acceptance, repeat use and specialist
review remain pending. See `DEPENDENCIES.md` for unresolved SDK and licensing items.
