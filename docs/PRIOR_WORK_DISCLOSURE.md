# Prior-work disclosure

The DBC/DAMM v2 research and private operational foundation existed before the
Crypto World's Fair competition opened on 14 September 2026.

Work recorded during the competition period included:

- keeper-ticket integration;
- streamed pool-state pricing;
- comparison evidence and failure semantics;
- execution-latency engineering in the private system;
- packaging this reviewer-facing validator, tests, dashboard, and disclosure.

This repository does not represent the entire private implementation as newly
created during the competition. File hashes identify inspected snapshots; they
do not independently prove authorship dates. Judges should assess the disclosed
competition-period work and the usefulness of the reviewer package on that
basis.

## 16 October 2026 follow-on release

The public v0.2 collector, versioned observations, deterministic replay,
transaction-level fixtures and interactive explorer were added on 16 October
2026 after the initial project submission. The dated build updates describe
continued development; they do not represent this work as completed before
the competition deadline or alter the organiser's submission rules.
