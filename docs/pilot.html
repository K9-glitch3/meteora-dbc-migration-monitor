What the evidence establishes

Readiness is a sampled reserve-to-threshold indicator, not a forecast or proof that migration is executable. A migrated account flag is separate from a finalized, identity-verified transaction. Quote checks compare supported captured states with Meteora's pinned SDK; they are not fills or profit estimates.

The default monitor reads a continuous mainnet feed from an independent remote server, even when my PC is off. It refreshes every five seconds, with separate upstream and sampled-readiness timestamps. Host, network, DNS or RPC failures can interrupt availability; there is no uptime SLA. Historical captures remain separate. Browser watchlist alerts require the page to be open, but the collector does not. Stale and unresolved evidence remains visible.
